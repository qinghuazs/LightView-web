LightView, for macOS

Double-click one photo.
Browse the whole folder.

A native Mac image viewer. Open any image and LightView turns its folder into a browsing session. Flip through with the arrow keys. No importing, no library, no network.

Three steps. No fourth.

Nothing to import, no library to build. The folder is the album.

Double-click any image

From Finder, the Desktop, Downloads or a USB drive. Dragging an image or a whole folder onto the window works too.

Press ← →

LightView has already scanned the folder, in the same order as Finder. A two-finger swipe on the trackpad works as well.

Close it when you are done

No database or cache folder is left on your disk. The next double-click is just as fast.

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

Written in Swift and AppKit with no third-party dependencies. The whole app is 2.4 MB.

Every format

JPEG, PNG, HEIC, GIF, WebP, TIFF, RAW and anything else macOS can decode. Animated images play automatically.

Big images stay smooth

Images are decoded at display size and only at full resolution past 100%. Neighbours are decoded ahead of time, so the next page is instant.

Zoom follows the pointer

⌘ plus scroll, or pinch, zooms around the pointer. Double-click toggles between fit-to-window and 100%.

Gallery and five sort orders

Press ⌘T for a thumbnail sidebar of the whole folder. Sort by name, date modified, date created, size or random, optionally reversed.

It notices folder changes

Files added, removed or renamed outside LightView show up immediately. Ejected drives resume when plugged back in; iCloud placeholders download on demand.

Undo a delete

⌘⌫ moves to the Trash, ⌘Z brings it back. No confirmation dialog by default; turn one on in Settings if you prefer.

Keyboard shortcuts

The arrow keys always turn the page, even while zoomed in. Pan by dragging or two-finger scrolling.

LightView runs in the macOS App Sandbox. The first time you open an image in a folder it asks you to grant access to that folder once, then remembers it. Nothing is read outside the folders you choose, and nothing ever leaves your Mac. See the privacy policy.

Make it the default viewer

  1. In Finder, right-click any image and choose "Get Info".
  2. Under "Open with", pick LightView.
  3. Click "Change All" so every image of that type opens in LightView.

Uninstall

  1. Drag LightView from Applications to the Trash.
  2. Settings live in ~/Library/Containers/com.lightview.app; delete that folder too if you like.
